## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the synthesis of cutting-edge neural information theory and haute-couture editorial curation. Designed for an advanced AI Semantic Communication system (DeepSC), the aesthetic rejects utilitarian, dashboard-cluttered SaaS conventions in favor of the poise, stillness, and authority found in museum monographs and archival print editions (Vogue, Kinfolk, Steidl).

The audience comprises research directors, machine learning purists, and executive architects who view deep semantic extraction not as mere data transmission, but as cognitive translation. The interface evokes quiet intellectual gravitas, archival permanence, and razor-sharp scientific precision.

The style is defined as **Editorial Minimalism with Architectural Rigor**:
- **Massive Negative Space**: Screen real estate is treated as tactile, heavy cotton-rag paper where emptiness conveys processing clarity and latent space.
- **Asymmetric Composition**: Editorial text pairings, offset metadata headers, and rhythmically varied columns establish a high-end cadence distinct from standard symmetric SaaS grids.
- **Exact Geometry**: Razor-sharp corners (0px radius) across all interactive elements, hairline architectural boundaries, and absolute typographic discipline.

## Colors

The palette references archival paper stocks, obsidian lithography ink, raw linen, and quiet metallic leaf. 

- **Primary (`#121212`)**: Obsidian ink. Used for master editorial headlines, primary telemetry metrics, focused states, and structural frames. Deep and matte, avoiding absolute `#000000` to prevent harsh visual clipping on paper-white tones.
- **Secondary (`#8C857B`)**: Muted Umbrian sand. Used for secondary body labels, system statuses, supporting telemetry tags, and structural sub-divisions.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880`)**: Muted champagne gold. Reserved strictly for semantic anomaly flags, semantic fidelity markers, transmission success indicators, and subtle focus underlines. Never applied over large surface fills.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5`)**: Natural matte ivory base. Supported by `#F5F2EB` for secondary layered sheets and `#E5E0D8` for hairline demarcations.

### Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base**: `#FBF9F5` (Matte Ivory)
- **Layered Sheet / Sidebar Base**: `#F5F2EB` (Warm Linen Ivory)
- **Hairline Dividers / Rules**: `#E5E0D8` (Warm Bone Hairline)
- **Deep Text / Headings**: `#121212` (Obsidian)
- **Secondary / Running Text**: `#242424` (Charcoal)
- **Muted Metadata / Axis Labels**: `#8C857B` (Muted Sand)
- **Accent Indicator / Active Semantic State**: `#C5A880` (Champagne Gold)

## Typography

The type system stages a dialogue between classical humanism (the high-fashion serif) and systematic cybernetics (the neutral sans-serif).

- **Playfair Display**: Dictates editorial presence. All major titles, semantic domain headers, latent space index names, and narrative summaries are set in light or regular weights with generous negative margins and tight display tracking.
- **Inter**: Serves as the high-resolution instrument font. Used for mathematical readouts, telemetry streams, semantic signal-to-noise ratios (SSNR), channel capacity ratings, and control interfaces.
- **Micro-Typography Rules**:
  - Telemetry and categorical labels must always be set in uppercase with tracking between `+0.14em` and `+0.2em`.
  - Body paragraphs in `body-lg` use a strict character line limit of 64 characters to replicate editorial column balance.
  - Numbers in data representations must use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve columnar vertical balance during live inference transmission.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model relies on an asymmetrical 12-column architectural grid with generous breathing room inspired by museum catalogs.

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - Desktop: 12-column fluid grid, 64px (`4rem`) outer margins, 32px (`2rem`) gutters. Main editorial narratives occupy 5 to 7 columns, leaving wide 2-to-3 column expanses for telemetry sidebars or uninhibited whitespace.
  - Tablet (768px - 1024px): 8-column layout, 32px (`2rem`) margins, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters.
  - Mobile (<768px): 4-column layout, 20px (`1.25rem`) margins, 16px (`1rem`) gutters.
- **Vertical Cadence**: Large section boundaries use `space-xl` (96px / 6rem) to force pause between analytical units. Telemetry readouts and inline labels use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for dense, scientific coherence.
- **Structural Borders**: Content regions are delineated by continuous or segmented 1px solid `#E5E0D8` hairline rules. Never rely on filled bounding boxes or elevation to separate content tiers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids ambient drop shadows, blurred elevations, and volumetric surface extrusions. The depth is strictly **planar and tactile**, reminiscent of physical sheets of high-grade paper laid upon one another.

- **Hairline Definition**: Layers are defined by single-pixel horizontal and vertical rules (`1px solid #E5E0D8`). Structural division is achieved through line rather than shade.
- **Tonal Stepping**: 
  - Ground Floor (Canvas): `#FBF9F5` (Ivory base).
  - Resting Layer (Data Panes, Modal Drawers, Telemetry Sheets): `#F5F2EB`.
  - Inset Well (Codeblocks, Semantic Matrix Inspects): `#EFECE4`.
- **Overlays & Drawers**: When inspect drawers or modal panels open, they slide strictly from the perimeter on 1px borders, accompanied by an ivory veil overlay: `rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.85)` with a delicate `backdrop-filter: blur(2px)`.

## Shapes

The geometry is unapologetically **sharp (`0`)**.

- Every perimeter, corner, input boundary, button, and data card terminates at an exact 90-degree right angle (0px border-radius).
- Circular or curved elements are strictly prohibited, with the sole exception of a live semantic status dot (4px circle).
- This pure angularity preserves the discipline of print bookmaking and scientific drafting tools.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Action**: Sharp rectangular button with a solid `#121212` obsidian fill, `#FBF9F5` ivory uppercase text (`label-data`), 0px radius, with 16px horizontal and 12px vertical padding. Hover state shifts fill to `#242424` with an instantaneous 150ms transition.
- **Secondary / Outline Action**: 1px `#121212` border, transparent background, `#121212` text. Hover state inverts background to `#121212` and text to `#FBF9F5`.
- **Text Action / Editorial Link**: Playfair Display italic or Inter uppercase with a continuous 1px bottom border placed 4px below the text baseline. Hover shifts border color to `#C5A880`.

### Data Cards & Content Panes
- Generic raised cards are replaced with **Editorial Panes**: flat sections surfaced in either `#FBF9F5` or `#F5F2EB` bounded by a top 1px rule in `#E5E0D8`.
- Panes lead with a micro-header: an all-caps telemetry label (`label-telemetry`) accompanied by a serial number (e.g., `SEC_04 // SEMANTIC_LATENT_MAP`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat horizontal underlined fields: No boxed inputs. A single bottom border of 1px `#8C857B`. On focus, the line turns `#121212` and thickens to 1.5px with no outline glow.
- Input text uses `body-md` in `#121212`, while floating labels utilize `label-telemetry` in `#8C857B`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- **Checkbox**: 14px x 14px square, 0px radius, 1px `#121212` border. Checked state renders a solid `#121212` inner square offset by a 2px ivory inset.
- **Radio**: 14px x 14px square rotated 45 degrees (diamond geometry), 1px `#121212` border. Active state features a solid `#121212` inner diamond.

### Chips & Badges
- Precision micro-tags with 0px radius, 1px border `#E5E0D8`, ivory background `#F5F2EB`, uppercase text in `label-telemetry` (`#8C857B`).
- Active or anomaly chips feature a 1px `#C5A880` border with matching champagne gold text.

### Telemetry & Signal Displays (Domain Specific)
- **Spectral Density & Channel Matrices**: Rendered via hairline SVG charts overlaid on a subtle 16px square grid of `#E5E0D8`. Data plots employ `#121212` as the primary curve, with semantic error margins indicated by `#C5A880` fills at 15% opacity.
- **Bitrate vs. Semantic Entropy Readouts**: Presented in side-by-side asymmetric columns with large `display-xl` numerals followed by microscopic uppercase units (`label-telemetry`) aligned directly to the cap-height of the figure.
